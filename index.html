How this calculator works

Your percentage for each subject is your entered marks divided by the maximum marks of the papers you entered. Where verified Cambridge grade-threshold data is available for your exact option, it is converted to a percentage of that option's published overall maximum and shown as a historical threshold estimate, alongside the average across whichever sessions are on record. Where no verified threshold data exists for a subject or option, this is stated plainly instead of guessing. Component maximum marks shown during entry are approximate where the exact figure could not be verified — check your own exam timetable/paper if you need the exact figure. Nothing here is an official Cambridge threshold unless labelled as such.
